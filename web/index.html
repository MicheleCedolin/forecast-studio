    What this page does. The forecasting core is the C++ library of Forecast Studio compiled to WebAssembly. Its ETS, ARIMA, TBATS and STL-based methods are ports of the R forecast package; on the NN5 Weekly sample the ETS and TBATS forecasts equal R's to 10 significant digits or more, and the ARIMA forecasts to within 0.0004%. The test part is the last h observations of each series.

    What it leaves out. The desktop edition adds the R engine, CatBoost, LightGBM, N-HiTS, Chronos-Bolt, prediction intervals, rolling origins, series features and the ranking-reliability analyses. Source code and desktop edition (GPL-3).
